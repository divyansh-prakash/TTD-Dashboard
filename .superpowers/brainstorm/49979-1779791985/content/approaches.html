A — Health strip on card header

A compact green row appears just below every platform header listing all healthy (zero-failure) categories as pills. Always visible — no click needed. Shows at a glance that those categories are covered.

Pros

  • Always visible, zero extra clicks
  • Immediate signal "these are safe"

Cons

  • Adds vertical height to every card
  • No detail (just names, not counts)

B — Healthy section inside expanded card

When a platform card is expanded, failing sub-accordions appear first (as today), followed by a "Healthy" divider row and green sub-rows for each zero-failure category with their served request counts.

Pros

  • Shows real numbers (how many served)
  • Same grid alignment — easy to compare
  • Doesn't clutter collapsed cards

Cons

  • Need to expand the card to see it

C — Toggle between Failures / All view

Each expanded platform card gets a small segmented toggle: "Failures" (default) shows only failing categories as today. "All" shows everything — failed and healthy together, color-coded red/green — plus served counts for the healthy ones.

Pros

  • Zero clutter by default
  • Unified view when you need it

Cons

  • Extra click to see healthy data
  • More state to manage per card
